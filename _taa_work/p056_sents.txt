Julia is 11.
She has got curly, brown hair, brown eyes and a big smile.
She's got a lot of friends at school.
All her classmates like her.
At lunchtime, Julia always goes to the school canteen and buys her lunch.
Her friend, Jack, doesn't buy any food.
He just drinks a glass of water.
Jack does this every day, and Julia is worried.
One day, she asks Jack, 'Are you hungry?'
'Yes,' says Jack.
'But you don't eat at school,' says Julia.
'I haven't got any money for lunch,' says Jack.
'My dad hasn't got a job and my mum's ill.
They've got lots of problems.
But don't worry about me – I'm OK.'
Julia thinks about this.
At home, she's got a big glass jar.
It's full of coins.
She wants to buy an electric scooter one day.
That night, Julia opens the jar, takes the coins and puts them into a bag.
Her mum sees her.
'Why are you doing that?' she asks.
'My friend Jack hasn't got any lunch money and I've got some money here.
This is for him.'
'That's very kind of you,' says her mum.
She feels happy that her daughter wants to do this.
The next day, Julia and her mum go and see her teacher.
They tell her about Jack and Julia gives her the money.
'This is for Jack's lunch.
Now, he can have lunch every day,' she says.
Julia hasn't got an electric scooter, but she's got a happy friend – Jack!

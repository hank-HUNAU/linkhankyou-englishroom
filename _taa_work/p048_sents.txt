Our school has a Robotics Club and it's brilliant.
I know this because I go to it!
So, what is a Robotics Club?
Easy – it's a club for building robots and I love robots.
Mr Thomas is the club leader.
He thinks of things for us to do and helps us build our robots.
He's really cool and very kind.
He never gets angry with us.
He teaches Music (not Design and Technology!), but he just loves everything to do with computers.
We often make our own robots, but we sometimes watch videos of other robots on the internet.
When our robot is ready, we use computer programs to make it do things.
Every year there is a competition for schools all over the country.
I feel really excited when we show our robots to students from other schools.
We never win, but that's not important.
The other students like watching our robot move.
It feels great.
I love Robotics Club.
It's a great place to meet new people.
You make so many friends and not just with people from school.
Robotics Club is for everyone.
We meet in the school hall every Monday at lunchtime and every Thursday after school.
Come and see us – we're always happy to see new people!

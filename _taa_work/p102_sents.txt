Everyone knows that dolphins are very intelligent animals.
But did you know that many scientists believe there is a special connection between dolphins and humans?
There are lots of stories about dolphins helping humans who are in danger.
Here are two of our favourites.
14-year-old Davide Cecchi was in his father's boat off the southeast coast of Italy when he had an accident and fell into the water.
Davide didn't know how to swim, and his father didn't see him fall, so he didn't stop the boat.
He shouted, but his dad didn't hear him.
Davide got very worried.
Then, very quickly, a dolphin came to help him.
It gave Davide a push back up to the top of the water, and the boy got on to the dolphin's back.
The dolphin took him back to the boat.
When Davide's father saw his son, he pulled him from the water.
The dolphin saved Davide's life.
In 2016, Adam Walker went to New Zealand to swim across the Cook Strait between the North and South Islands.
He was swimming when he saw a large dark shape below him.
He knew that it was a great white shark and his life was in danger.
Adam knew he didn't have a lot of time.
A few seconds later, a group of ten dolphins joined him and swam in a circle between him and the shark.
The circle of dolphins kept Adam safe.
After half an hour, the shark left, but the dolphins swam with him for another hour.

Culture note
Jess Glynne is a singer-songwriter 
from England. Born in 1989, she rose 
to fame in 2014 and was a featured 
artist on the singles Rather Be by 
Clean Bandit and My Love by Route 
94, both UK number ones. Her first 
studio album, I Cry When I Laugh, 
which was released in 2015, debuted 
at number one in the UK Albums 
Chart. She is the first British female 
solo artist to have seven number one 
singles in the UK Singles Chart and in 
2019, Forbes magazine considered 
her to be one of the most influential 
people under 30.

 Homework
Ask students to keep a diary for two 
or three days and make a note of the 
music they listened to, where they 
were when they listened to it and 
whether they enjoyed it. As a follow-
up in the next lesson, ask students to 
describe their diary to a partner. For 
example. It’s six o’clock on Thursday. 
I’m listening to … on the bus.

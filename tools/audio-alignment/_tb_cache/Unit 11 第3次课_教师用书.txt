Steller’s sea cow was a large animal that 
lived in the cold Arctic waters around 
Alaska. It grew up to nine metres long. 
Because it was so big, it couldn’t go under 
the water. It ate the plants on top of the 
sea. It wasn’t a very dangerous animal – it 
was quite friendly. Because of this, it was 
very easy to kill. Europeans first found the 
sea cow in 1741. 27 years later they killed 
the last one.
The great auk was a large seabird that lived 
in the North Atlantic. It was a bird, but it 
couldn’t fly. It could swim under water really 
well. It caught and ate fish when it dived 
under the water. Its feathers were very soft 
and people used them to make pillows for 
their beds. Because of this, people killed 
the birds. The last great auk died on the 
island of Eldey in 1835.
The Tasmanian tiger was only found in 
Australia, Tasmania and New Guinea. It got 
its name because it had stripes like a tiger, 
but it was not a member of the cat family. 
Farmers believed Tasmanian tigers killed 
their sheep, so they paid people to kill 
them. Many of the tigers died after getting 
ill from diseases they caught from other 
animals. The last Tasmanian tiger died in 
Hobart Zoo in 1936.

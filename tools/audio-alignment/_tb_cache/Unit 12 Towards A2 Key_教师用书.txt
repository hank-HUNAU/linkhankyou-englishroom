3  12.08 In A2 Key for Schools Listening Part 3, candidates have to listen 
to an informal conversation and answer five three-option multiple-choice 
questions. This task includes questions on the opinions and attitudes of the 
speakers, and it tests understanding of detailed information.
Encourage your students to do the following when they approach this task:
• Read and listen to the instructions to understand the context of the 
conversation.
• On the first listening, try to get the gist and choose the best option for 
each question.
• On the second listening, check all the answers carefully, focusing on 
specific information.

 Homework
Ask students to create a poster 
showing what you should or should 
not do when you have a new student 
in your class. Draw two columns 
on the board, one with OK and the 
other with Not OK. Ask students for 
some ideas. Encourage them to use 
their imaginations and to present the 
information in an interesting way, 
using colours, symbols and drawings 
if necessary. If there is space, display 
the posters on the walls of the 
classroom for students to circulate 
and look at each other’s work. This 
could be done as a digital poster if 
students have access to computers.

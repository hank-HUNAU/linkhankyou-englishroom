Language note Present continuous
We don’t repeat the main verb in short answers, e.g. Are you listening? Yes, I 
am. NOT: Yes, I am listening.

Gamelan is the traditional music of the Javanese, Sudanese and Balinese 
peoples of Indonesia. It is made up of mainly of percussion instruments. 
The main instruments are a set of hand-played drums called kendhang and 
xylophone-type instruments which are played with hammers. Other gamelan 
instruments include bamboo flutes and the rebab which is a bowed instrument.
Drums range in style and sound all over Africa. Many consist of a skin stretched 
over the open end of a frame. The most well-known are the djembe – a goblet-
shaped drum, played with bare hands – and the talking drum – an hour-glass 
shaped drum with a drumhead at each end. Most African music relies on fast-
paced, upbeat drumming which often uses two or more rhythms simultaneously. 
Bagpipes are a woodwind instrument which use a bag to feed a constant flow 
of air through enclosed reeds. Although the Scottish Great Highland bagpipes 
are the best known, they also occur throughout large parts of Europe, northern 
Africa and western Asia. Bagpipes have been played for centuries.

Culture notes
Mariachi is a genre of music from 
western Mexico. The modern-day 
mariachi group is usually made up of 
up to eight violins, two trumpets and 
at least one guitar. All players take 
turns singing.

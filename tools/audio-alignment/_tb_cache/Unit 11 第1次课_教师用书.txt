7 SPEAKINGSPEAKING  Work in pairs and compare your ideas from Exercise 6.
14-year-old Davide Ceci was in his father’s boat off the south-east coast of Italy 
when he had an accident and fell into the water. Davide didn’t know how to swim 
and his father didn’t see him fall, so he didn’t stop the boat. He shouted, but his 
dad didn’t hear him. Davide got very worried. Then, very quickly, a dolphin came 
to help him. It gave Davide a push back up to the top of the water and the boy got 
onto the dolphin’s back. The dolphin took him back to the boat. When Davide’s 
father saw his son, he pulled him from the water. The dolphin saved Davide’s life!
In 2016, Adam Walker went to New Zealand to swim across the Cook Strait 
between the North and South Islands. He was swimming when he saw a large 
dark shape below him. He knew that it was a great white shark and his life was 
in danger. Adam knew he didn’t have a lot of time. A few seconds later, a group 
of ten dolphins joined him and swam in a circle between him and the shark. 
The circle of dolphins kept Adam safe. After half an hour, the shark left, but the 
dolphins swam with him for another hour.
Everyone knows that dolphins are very intelligent animals. But did you know 
that many scientists believe there is a special connection between dolphins 
and humans? There are lots of stories about dolphins helping humans who 
are in danger. Here are two of our favourites.

Culture notes
Italy is in south-central Europe. It is 
a peninsula and several surrounding 
islands and is the third-most 
populated member state of the 
European Union with about 60 million 
inhabitants. The Roman Empire, 
centred on the ancient city of Rome, 
was established in 27 BCE and 
continued until 476 CE (in Western 
Europe). It was among the most 
powerful economic, cultural, political 
and military forces in world history. 
At the height of its power, it covered 
five million square kilometres.

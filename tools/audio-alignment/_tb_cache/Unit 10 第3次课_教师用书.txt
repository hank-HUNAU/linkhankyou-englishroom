Culture notes
Dee Caffari is a British sailor. In 2006, after 178 days at sea, she became the first 
woman to sail solo, non-stop, around the world. In June 2009, she set a new 
record for circumnavigating Britain and Ireland, beating the existing time by 17 
hours. She has sailed around the world six times and is the only woman to have 
sailed non-stop around the world a total of three times.
Jordan Romero is mountain climber and world record holder from the US. In 
2011, he became the youngest climber in the world to complete the Seven 
Summits – the highest mountains in each of the seven continents.
Gertrude Ederle was a competitive swimmer from the United States. She was 
born in 1905 and was an Olympic champion and world record holder in five 
events. She held 29 US national and world records from 1921 until 1925. In 
1926, she became the first woman to swim across the English Channel. Due 
to childhood measles, she was almost completely deaf by the 1940s when she 
began teaching swimming to deaf children.

Activity idea Mixed-ability
Stronger classes: Students can create dialogues spontaneously. Encourage 
them to go on to create new dialogues with other ideas of their own, using the 
expressions for making suggestions.
Weaker classes: Ask students to write down their dialogues before practising them.

Language note some / any
Point out that we use some to refer to an indefinite number and any when we 
don’t know the number or when there are none. Give further examples to clarify 
this use of some and any, e.g. There are some books in the cupboard. There 
aren’t any pens on the table.

Language note The imperative
The imperative can sound rude in some circumstances, so we often add please 
before or after the phrase.
Activity idea Extension
A fun way to practise imperatives is 
to play ‘Simon Says’. Say Simon says 
please … and then an imperative (e.g. 
Simon says please stand up. Simon 
says please sit down.). If you do not 
say Simon says, students must NOT 
do the action. If they do, they are out 
of the game. They are also out if they 
do the wrong thing (e.g. they lift their 
left hand when you tell them to lift 
their right hand). Continue until only 
one student is left.

DANCES
THE WHIRLING DERVISH
The Whirling Dervish is a dance from 
Turkey. There is a group of twelve men who 
dance. They wear traditional costumes with 
big skirts. They often all wear white, but 
sometimes the skirts are different colours. 
There is another group of twelve musicians. 
They play traditional Turkish instruments. 
As/uni00A0the music plays, the dancers spin around 
in circles, getting faster and faster. The dance 
is very popular/uni00A0with/uni00A0tourists.
IRISH DANCING
There are lots of Irish dances, but perhaps the 
most famous one is the ‘step dance’ . One or more 
dancers perform the step dance. The dancers only 
move their legs. They move them very quickly. 
The top half of the body doesn’t move. Dancers 
usually wear traditional Irish costumes. The 
music they dance to is played by an Irish folk 
band. They play instruments like the fiddle and 
the whistle.

Culture notes
The Tango is a dance that originated 
in Argentina and Uruguay in the 
1800s. The dance began with 
enslaved African populations in 
poor harbour areas. Although many 
variations of this dance currently 
exist around the world, it is always 
danced with a partner. In 2009, 
Tango was recognised by UNESCO 
as an ‘Intangible Cultural Heritage’ 
of Argentina and Uruguay.
The whirling dance (or Sufi 
whirling) is a dance from Turkey. 
It is best known in the West by the 
performances of dervishes. It is part 
of a formal ceremony known as the 
Sama and is only one of the many 
Sufi ceremonies performed to try 
to reach a spiritual trance. 
Irish dance is the collective term 
for several traditional dance forms 
that originated from Ireland in the 
17th century. The dances, which can 
be performed by individuals and in 
groups, were standardised in the 
early 20th century for competitions 
and have become popular in many 
parts of the world. Most modern Irish 
dancers wear elaborate dresses, large 
wigs and makeup for performance 
and competition purposes.

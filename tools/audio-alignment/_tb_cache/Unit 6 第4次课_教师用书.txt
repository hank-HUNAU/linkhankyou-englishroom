Welcoming people around the world
How do you say hello to someone you know? Do you 
smile? Do you touch the other person? Here are some 
ideas/uni00A0for travellers. This is how people in different 
countries welcome each other. Do you do different things 
in your/uni00A0country?
In many countries in Asia, people bow their heads when 
they meet each other. This shows respect. In India, Nepal 
and Bangladesh, people bring their hands together in front 
of their heart and say ‘Namaste’ .
When you greet a grandmother or grandfather or another 
older person in Turkey, you kiss their right hand and 
then place it on your forehead. They do this in the 
Philippines,/uni00A0too.
The Maori people in New Zealand rub noses together 
when they meet. This is called the ‘hongi’ .
In Malaysia, you put your hand on your heart 
and nod your head. Sometimes you also take 
the person’s hands in your hands first.
In Western countries, many people shake 
hands when they meet. Sometimes 
they just smile and say something like 
‘Hello!’ or ‘Hi!’ In the US and in other 
countries, some people like using a 
‘fist bump’ to greet another person. 
Both people make a fist, push their 
arm forward slowly and touch 
each other’s fists.
In many countries around 
the world, friends kiss each 
other on the cheek. In some 
countries they kiss one 
cheek, in others they kiss 
both cheeks and in some 
they kiss cheeks three times.

The Māori are the indigenous people of New Zealand’s two main islands, who 
arrived in waka (canoe) from eastern Polynesia in the 14th century. Their culture 
developed differently from other Polynesian islands and they have their own 
language and traditions.
New Zealand (in Māori: Aotearoa) is in the South Pacific Ocean. The country is 
made up of two main islands – the North Island (Te Ika-a-Māui) and the South 
Island (Te Waipounamu) – and around 600 smaller islands. The capital city is 
Wellington, but its largest city is Auckland, both of which are on the North 
Island. New Zealand was the last large habitable land to be settled by humans.
Malaysia is in Southeast Asia. It is made up of two regions which are separated 
by the South China Sea. Kuala Lumpur is the national capital and largest city, 
while Putrajaya is the seat of the federal government. The country is multi-ethnic 
and multi-cultural. About half the population is Malay, with large minorities of 
Chinese, Indian and indigenous peoples.

Culture notes
India is in South Asia. It is the seventh-
largest country in the world by area 
and has the second-biggest population. 
The capital is New Delhi and the largest 
city is Mumbai. 
Nepal is a country between India 
and China in South Asia. The 
Himalayas cover a lot of the country, 
and it has eight of the world’s ten 
tallest mountains, including Mount 
Everest. The capital and largest city 
is Kathmandu. Nepal is a multi-ethnic 
country, with Nepali as the official 
language.
Bangladesh is a country bordering 
north-east India. It has a population 
of more than 162 million people. The 
capital and largest city is Dhaka.
Turkey is a country which spans both 
Europe and Asia. For centuries, 
Turkey was at the centre of two great 
empires – the Byzantine Empire and 
the Ottoman Empire. The largest city 
is Istanbul, which has parts in both 
Europe and Asia. The capital city 
Ankara, which is in the centre of the 
country, is in Asia.
The Philippines is in Southeast Asia. 
It is made up of about 7,641 islands 
in the Pacific Ocean. The capital city 
is Manila, but more people live in 
Quezon City.

Parks around the world
A  Villa Borghese, Rome, Italy
 Villa Borghese is a big public park. There are 
beautiful gardens, trees and some fountains, too. 
People also call it ‘the park of museums’ because 
there are many interesting museums there.
B  Golden Gate Park, San Francisco, US
 Everyone knows the Golden Gate Bridge, but did 
you know that there is a Golden Gate Park in San 
Francisco, too? It’s very big (4.8km long, almost 
1km wide) and at one end you can see the sea.
C   Chapultepec Park, Mexico City, Mexico
 Chapultepec Park is an important green space in 
this big city. It has a lake and a hill with a castle 
on it. People in Mexico City love going there.
D   El Parque del Buen Retiro, Madrid, Spain
 In Madrid there is a very big park called Buen 
Retiro. There are trees and paths and a lovely 
rose garden, too. There is also a beautiful glass 
palace.
E  The Iguana Park, Guayaquil, Ecuador
 The park’s real name is Parque Seminario, but 
everyone calls it the Iguana Park because it is full 
of iguanas. The iguanas are very friendly. People 
go there and feed them.
F  Mount Faber Park, Singapore
 On a mountain near Singapore, there is a park 
called Mount Faber. You can get there by cable 
car. In the park you can walk on a long path, or on 
an amazing bridge. You get a great view of the city.

Parque del Buen Retiro in Madrid, Spain is more usually known as El Retiro. It is 
one of the largest parks of the capital, measuring 141 hectares and until the late 
19th century belonged to the Spanish Monarchy. It is in the centre of the city, very 
close to other famous sights such as the Puerta de Alcalá and the Prado Museum.
Parque Seminario in Guayaquil, Ecuador is also known as Parque Bolívar, as it has 
a monument to Simón Bolívar, or Parque de las Iguanas. It is a small and traditional 
public urban park in the centre of the town and is famous for having around 350 
iguanas of various species, some of which can be one metre in length. The park 
was opened in 1895 by the President of Ecuador, General Eloy Alfaro.
Mount Faber is a hill about 94 metres high in the town of Bukit Merah in 
Singapore. The park there is a popular tourist destination, as there is a 
panoramic view of the harbour area. You can reach the top by a cable car 
system which connects to Harbourfront and Sentosa.

Culture notes
Villa Borghese gardens in Rome, 
Italy is the city’s third largest public 
park and contains a number of 
buildings, museums and attractions. 
The gardens were built by Flaminio 
Ponzio from sketches by the owner, 
Cardinal Scipione Borghese, who 
used it to house his art collection. 
Today’s gardens were recreated in 
the 19th century.
Golden Gate Park in San Francisco, 
United States is a large urban 
public park. It is 411 hectares and 
rectangular, so similar in shape 
to Central Park in New York City, 
although Golden Gate Park is 20 
percent bigger. Golden Gate is the 
third most-visited city park in the 
United States with 24 million visitors 
annually.
Chapultepec in Mexico City, 
Mexico is also known as ‘Bosque 
de Chapultepec’. It has around 15 
million visitors a year and is one of 
the largest city parks in the Americas. 
It is 686 hectares and its trees are 
important for the rebalancing of the 
oxygen levels in the Valley of Mexico. 
The park has three sections. Most of 
the park’s attractions including the 
castle, the Chapultepec Zoo and the 
Museum of Anthropology are in the 
oldest part.

 Homework
If you’re short on time, students can do Writing Exercise 4 for homework. If you 
have done this in class, ask students to exchange their brochures with another 
student for them to read at home and give feedback on content and structure. 
Students could check if the brochure uses the same structure as the model and if 
all the points have been covered. They should also decide which the most/least 
interesting part of the brochure is.

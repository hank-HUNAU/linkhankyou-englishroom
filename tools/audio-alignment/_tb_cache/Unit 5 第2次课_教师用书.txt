Activity idea Extension
Ask students to think of two more things that make them happy. Write the 
following questions on the board and ask students to work individually to make 
notes on their answers for each of the two things they have chosen: What 
makes you happy? When and where do you do it? Who do you do it with? Why 
does it make you happy?
Monitor and input vocabulary as required. When the majority of students have 
finished, divide the class into small groups for students to discuss their answers, 
asking each other the questions on the board.

Activity idea Disappearing sentences
Write out the dialogue in Exercise 5 on the board. Put students in AB pairs and 
get them to practise the conversation. Cover a small section of the dialogue, 
beginning from the right-hand side of the screen or board. Students practise the 
dialogue again, including the part which they have to remember.
Students have to try to remember the part they can no longer see. Repeat 
the activity again and again, covering more and more of the dialogue each 
time. Continue until all the dialogue is covered and students are practising the 
dialogue from memory.

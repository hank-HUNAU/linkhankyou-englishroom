9 SPEAKINGSPEAKING  Tell your partner your story. 
Use the sequencing words from the Key 
Language box to structure your story.
Toby: Are there snakes in the forest? Are you sure?
Nathan: Yes, I’m sure. It was on the path. I stopped. I didn’t 
do anything. Then it just went away. I was afraid.
Lena: Poor you! I hate snakes, too.
Toby: Why were you afraid? It’s just a snake. A snake can’t 
kill you.
Lena: Some snakes can! Anyway, it’s OK to be afraid 
sometimes.
Nathan: Thanks, Lena. Anyway, that’s why my weekend was 
just OK.
Lena: Hey Nathan. How are you? Did you have 
a good weekend?
Nathan: Well, sort of. I mean … it was OK.
Lena: You don’t sound very sure! What happened?
Toby: Yeah, come on, tell us.
Nathan: All right. Well, on Sundays we usually go for a walk, 
my family and I. But yesterday, I went alone.
Toby: Where did you go?
Nathan: I went to the forest – you know, outside town. 
It’s really nice there. And I started walking, when 
suddenly …
Lena: Yes? Suddenly what?
Nathan: Well, in front of me, there was a snake.

Activity idea Self-correction
If students have access to a recording facility (using their mobile phones, for 
example), they could record their story and play it back to check their own 
pronunciation and intonation. They can then repeat the exercise and listen 
for improvements.

 Homework
Ask students to write six positive and negative sentences about themselves 
and members of their family using the irregular verbs in the past simple. As an 
alternative, they could write five true sentences and one false one. Give your 
own examples to get them started. As a follow-up in the next lesson, they could 
work in pairs and each partner guesses which of the sentences is untrue.

about their statement and make a list 
of reasons for their opinion. Students 
present their opinions in open class.

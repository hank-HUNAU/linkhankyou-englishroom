Lucy: Hey, Liz. What’s wrong?
Liz: I’ve got a cold.
Claudio: So? Everyone gets a cold sometimes. What’s the 
problem?
Liz: The problem? The problem is, there’s the school 
concert tomorrow. And I’m the singer!
Claudio: I’m sure you can still sing.
Liz: No way, Claudio! Listen to my voice!
Lucy: She’s right. This really is a problem. Oh, Liz, 
I’m sorry.
Claudio: Don’t worry, Liz. We’re here to help you.
Lucy: How, Claudio?
Claudio: I don’t know!
Liz: Just a minute. I’ve got an idea. Lucy, you can sing, 
can’t you?
Lucy: A little, Liz. But I can’t sing with a band.
Claudio: Oh, come on, Lucy. You can do it. You’re a great 
singer.
Lucy: OK, I’ll do it for you, Liz. But this is crazy! OK, what 
are the songs?
Liz: I’ll tell you later. You’re the best, Lucy!

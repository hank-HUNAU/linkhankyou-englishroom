Activity idea Mixed ability
Weaker classes: Give students two minutes (or more if necessary) to prepare 
the new dialogues in Exercise 9.
Stronger classes: Students should be able to create dialogues without 
preparation time.
Activity idea Extension
If students have access to a recording facility (on their mobile phones, for 
example), they could record their dialogues and play them back to check their 
own pronunciation and intonation. They can then repeat the exercise and listen 
out for improvements.

Activity idea Extension
Students could role play a dialogue with Oliver or Marta and a friend. Divide the 
class into four groups: A (Oliver) and B (Oliver’s friend); C (Marta) and D (Marta’s 
friend). Ask groups B and D to look at the relevant text again and write down 
five supportive phrases to say to either Oliver or Marta. Ask groups A and C to 
look at the relevant text again and find key information about Oliver or Marta 
in preparation for a role play. Monitor to make sure all the students are making 
notes and help with ideas if necessary. Put the students in A/B and C/D pairs 
and give them time to act out the role play.

 Homework
Ask students to keep a diary for two 
or three days, with a record of times 
when they needed to be patient. Did 
they manage to be patient? If so, 
how? If not, what could they have 
done to be more patient?
Activity idea Extension
Ask students to write a list of three 
things they can wait for (e.g. my 
birthday). As an alternative, they 
could write five true sentences 
and one false one. Give your own 
examples to get them started. 
Then ask them to work in pairs and 
each partner guesses which of the 
sentences is untrue.

Culture note
Research conducted by Meerkat Meals in 2019 reported that the average British 
person dines out at least once a month, with six in ten choosing to eat at the 
same places every time. The research found that Caribbean was the cuisine most 
British people would most like to try, with a fifth of diners saying it was on top 
of their ‘to try’ list. The top ten cuisines British people choose when dining out 
were:
1 Pub/Café food
2 Italian
3 Chinese
4 Indian
5 American
6 Mexican
7 Thai
8 Spanish
9 Mediterranean
10 French
It also found British people spend more than five hours deciding where to eat 
each year and prefer to eat out when somebody else chooses the restaurant.

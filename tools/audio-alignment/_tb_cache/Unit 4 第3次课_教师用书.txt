Language note Large numbers
Point out that there is no and between the thousands and hundreds where the 
comma goes (one thousand, two hundred) but there is an and between the 
thousands and tens or ones (one thousand and fifty / one thousand and five).

Activity idea Extension
Divide the class into groups of three. Students take turns to give each other sums 
using prices. The other two students have to race to be first to get the correct 
answer, for which they get one point. This can also be played as a whole-class 
competition, with you giving the sums and students competing to be first to get 
the answer.
Language note Prices
Prices are said in various ways. For 
example, for £2.50, the correct usage 
is two pounds fifty. However, it is also 
common to hear the colloquial forms 
two pound fifty and simply two fifty.

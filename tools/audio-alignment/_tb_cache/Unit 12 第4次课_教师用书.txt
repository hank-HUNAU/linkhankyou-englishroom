The big, blue Amfibus certainly makes a big 
splash! It’s in Rotterdam and it takes people on 
tours of the city. It’s cool. It can travel on land and 
in the water. It is more expensive than water taxis, 
but it is more exciting, too. It is slower in the water 
than on land. It goes at 8kmph in the water and 
96kmph on land. The tour lasts for an hour and it 
costs 27.50 euros.
Ankara’s cable car is cheaper than other forms of 
public transport and it is healthier because it doesn’t 
pollute the air. It is also easier and quicker than 
travelling by car because there are no traffic jams 
in/uni00A0the sky! It travels 60 metres above the ground and 
passengers can get great views of the city.
The Central-Mid-Levels Escalator is 800 metres 
long. Escalators are usually inside a shopping 
centre or an airport, but not this one! It’s outside. 
It’s a great way to travel round this city of hills. 
And it’s free! There are 14 places where you can 
get on and off. The escalators travel down the hill 
from 6am to 10am so people can travel to work. 
They travel up the hill from 10am to midnight.
Now you can travel in a submarine! This submarine 
carries tourists. It is a safe and comfortable way to 
see the colourful fish and the plants and rocks under 
our seas. The submarine can carry 48 people and 
it has 11 large windows on each side. It travels 
down 60 metres under the sea.

Lanzarote (pronounced in Spanish as /lanθaˈɾote/) is a Spanish island in the 
north Atlantic Ocean and the most easterly of the Canary Islands. It is located 
approximately 125 kilometres off the north coast of Africa and 1,000 kilometres 
from the Iberian Peninsula. Lanzarote is the fourth-largest of the islands in the 
archipelago and the third most populated. The island’s capital is Arrecife. In 
1993, the island was declared a Biosphere Reserve by UNESCO.
Tenerife (pronounced in Spanish as /teneˈɾife/) is the largest and most populated 
island of the Canary Islands. About 43 percent of the total population of the 
Canary Islands live on Tenerife. The oldest university in the Canaries – the 
University of La Laguna – was founded here in 1792 in San Cristóbal de La 
Laguna. The city of La Laguna is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The Macizo 
de Anaga mountain range on the island has the largest number of endemic species 
in Europe and has been a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2015.

Culture notes
An amphibious vehicle is one that is 
suitable for both land and water.
Rotterdam is a city in the 
Netherlands. It is a key logistic and 
economic centre, and Europe’s 
largest seaport, with access to 
several major European waterways. 
The Rotterdam–The Hague 
metropolitan area is one of the 
largest in the European Union and 
has a population of approximately 
2.7 million and over 180 nationalities.
Hong Kong is an area of the People’s 
Republic of China. With a territory of 
1,107 km2 and over 7 million people, 
Hong Kong is one of the most 
densely populated areas in the world. 
Hong Kong has become one of the 
world’s most significant financial 
centres and commercial ports.
Ankara is the capital of Turkey. 
It is in central Anatolia, which is 
the part of the country in Asia. 
It has a population of more than 
4.5 million people, making it the 
second largest city in Turkey after 
Istanbul (the former imperial capital). 
Archaeological evidence indicates 
the area has been inhabited at least 
since the Stone Age and architecture 
of the present-day city reflects its

Culture notes
Wisconsin is in the Midwest of the US. Its name is Native American and means 
‘meeting of the waters’. It’s home to two of the largest lakes in the US – Lake 
Michigan and Lake Superior – and thousands of inland lakes and waterways.
Lake Superior is the world’s largest freshwater lake by surface area. Located on 
the border of the United States and Canada, it is the largest of the Great Lakes. 
The indigenous Ojibwe people of the area call it gichi-gami.

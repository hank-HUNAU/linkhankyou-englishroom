Culture note
An extracurricular activity is a student activity outside the normal curriculum of 
school, college or university education. Such activities are usually voluntary, social 
and with similar age groups. Examples are: Art club, Band/Orchestra, Choir, Code 
club, Football and Netball. Although some are provided by schools for free or at 
a minimal cost, many are paid-for activities such as music lessons and gymnastics 
classes. Whether children take part in the activities, or not, often depends on 
where the school is and the social background of the children. Children aged ten 
to 15 from wealthier families are much more likely to take part in every type of 
activity, especially music and sport, compared to those from poorer backgrounds, 
where there is a 20 percent participation gap.

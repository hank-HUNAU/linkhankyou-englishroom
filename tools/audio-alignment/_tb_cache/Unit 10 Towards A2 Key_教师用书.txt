1 In A2 Key for Schools Reading and 
Writing Part 2, candidates have to 
read three short texts on the same 
topic followed by seven questions. 
They have to match each question 
to one of the texts. This part of 
the exam tests locating specific 
information by reading quickly and 
understanding detail by reading 
carefully. The Student’s Book has 
a simplified version with only two 
short texts and six questions, but 
the skills required are the same.
Encourage your students to do the 
following when they approach this 
task:
• Read each question to find out 
what information they need to 
look for in the texts.
• Quickly read the texts and try 
to find the parts relevant to 
each question.
• Read the relevant piece of text 
carefully to check whether it 
answers the question.
• Before choosing the answer, 
check that the other texts 
don’t contain anything that 
could answer the questions. 
If one of them does, decide 
which text best matches the 
question.

2  10.10 In A2 Key for Schools 
Listening Part 4, candidates have 
to listen to five short monologues 
or dialogues, each with a question 
and three options, then choose 
the option which best answers 
the question based on what they 
have heard. This part of the exam 
tests understanding of the gist of a 
monologue or dialogue containing 
neutral or less formal language 
and may include the correct 
identification of attitudes, opinions 
and agreement. The Student’s 
Book has a simplified version with 
only four dialogues, but the skills 
required are the same.
Encourage your students to do the 
following when they approach this 
task:
• Read all the questions and 
options.
• On the first listening, get the 
gist meaning and choose the 
best option.
• On the second listening, check 
that the answer is correct.

 Homework
Tell students they are going to prepare a survey to find out about healthy eating 
habits. Ask students to write statements like those in Exercise 13. Point out 
that all the statements should score 1 for a healthy habit and 5 for an unhealthy 
habit. Students ask the questions to members of their family and give a score. 
As a follow-up, students could use the information to write a short text on the 
eating habits of their family or present their findings to the class and find out 
who has the healthiest diet.

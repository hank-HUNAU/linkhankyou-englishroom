1 In A2 Key for Schools Reading 
and Writing Part 4, candidates 
have to read a short text with 
six numbered spaces, then 
decide which of the three words 
provided belongs in each gap. 
This part of the exam tests 
understanding of words within 
a context. In this example, an 
example is given so students only 
need to answer five questions.
Encourage your students to do 
the following when they approach 
this task:
• Skim the text to find out the 
topic and general meaning.
• Work through the six 
questions, reading the whole 
sentence to choose the 
correct word to complete 
each gap.
• After choosing an answer, 
check the other two options 
and decide why they are 
wrong.
• Read the completed text 
again to make sure it makes 
sense.

Encourage your students to do 
the following when they approach 
this task:
• Read and listen to the 
instructions, then read the 
title and the questions and 
think about the context.

and write down the necessary 
information.

check that the answers are 
correct. If the word was 
spelled in the recording, the 
answer must also be spelled 
correctly.

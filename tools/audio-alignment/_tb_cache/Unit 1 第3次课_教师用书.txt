Culture notes
Ariana Grande is a singer-songwriter and actress from the United States. Born 
in 1993, she began her career at the age of 15 in the Broadway musical 13. She 
rose to fame in 2010 playing the role of Cat Valentine in the TV series Victorious. 
Her debut album, Yours Truly, was released in 2013 and entered the US Billboard 
200 chart at number one. She is currently the only artist to have their first four 
number one songs debut at the top. In 2016 and 2019, Time magazine named 
her as one of the 100 most influential people in the world.
Shawn Mendes is a singer-songwriter from Canada. Born in 1998, he gained 
a following in 2013 after posting song covers on a video-sharing application. 
Mendes released a self-titled debut EP in 2014 and his debut studio album 
Handwritten in 2015. Entering the chart at number one, the album made Mendes 
one of only five artists ever to debut at number one before the age of 18. His 
third album, also self-titled, was released in 2018 and went straight to number 
one, making him the third-youngest artist to achieve three number one albums.

Language note Indefinite articles
The indefinite articles a and an are used before a singular noun. They show that 
what is referred to is not already known to the speaker/writer: Do you have a 
cat? This can be seen in contrast to the which is a definite article and shows that 
what is referred to is already know to the speaker/writer: Where is the cat?
We used a /ə/ before a consonant sound: a car, a T-shirt, a big apple. We use an 
/ən/ before a vowel sound: an old car, an expensive T-shirt, an apple.
Note that some words that begin with a vowel letter in writing have a consonant 
sound and so do not use an: a university, a one-year-old child and vice versa.

Language note can (ability)
If students produce sentences like I don’t know speak English because of the 
way their own language works, remind them of the function of can.
Can and can’t are followed by the infinitive without to, e.g. I can sing not I can 
to sing.

Language note Telling the time
We can say quarter past/to or a quarter past/to. We can say half past, but not 
a half past.
As well as What’s the time?, we can also say What time is it? or Have you got 
the time?
Activity idea Mixed-ability
Weaker classes: Before listening, in Exercise 11, check as a class that students 
know how to tell the time on each clock.
Stronger classes: Students may like to work in pairs and try to answer the 
questions from memory before listening to the audio again.

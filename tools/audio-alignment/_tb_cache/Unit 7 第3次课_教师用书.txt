Culture note
Basketball is a game where two teams of five players each try to score points by 
shooting a ball through a hoop about 3 metres above the ground. It is played 
on a court, which is divided into two main sections by the mid-court line. There 
is also an arc marked around each hoop called the three-point arc. The ball is 
moved down the court toward the basket by passing or dribbling. The team 
with the ball is called the offense. When a team puts the ball through the hoop 
from inside the three-point arc, they score two points and the ball goes to the 
other team. If a basket is made from outside the three-point arc, then the team 
score three points. A free throw is worth one point. Each game is divided into 
sections and all levels have two halves. Depending at which level the game 
is being played, the game can be different lengths: in college, each half is 
20-minutes long. In high school and below, the halves are divided into eight-
minute (and sometimes, six-minute) quarters. In professional games, quarters 
are twelve minutes long. There is a gap of several minutes between halves. 
Gaps between quarters are relatively short. At half-time, the teams switch goals. 
The winning team is the one with the most points at the final whistle.

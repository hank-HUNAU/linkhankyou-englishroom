1 In A2 Key for Schools Reading 
and Writing Part 1, candidates 
read six short texts. These could 
be emails, notices, signs or text 
messages. Candidates will also 
find three sentences next to each 
text. Students have to choose 
which sentence matches the 
meaning of the text. This part of 
the exam tests the candidates’ 
understanding of different types 
of short text.
Encourage your students to do 
the following when they approach 
this task:
• Look at the visual information 
to help identify what type of 
text it is.
• Read the text to find out more 
about the context.
• After looking at the text, read 
the three possible options.
• Compare each option with 
the text before choosing an 
answer.
• Read the texts again to 
confirm the correct answer.

2 In A2 Key for Schools Reading 
and Writing Part 6, candidates 
have to write a message of 25 
words or more, for example a 
note or email. This part of the 
exam tests the candidates’ ability 
to write short texts with a real 
communicative purpose.
Encourage your students to do 
the following when they approach 
this task:
• Read the instructions carefully, 
to identify what kind of 
message is required and who 
it is for, and what kind of 
information is needed.
• Respond to all three 
prompts.
• Write a rough draft of the 
message before writing the 
final answer.

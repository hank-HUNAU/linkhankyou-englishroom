1  8.08 In A2 Key for Schools 
Listening Part 1, there are five 
short recordings, each with a 
question and three images. 
Candidates have to listen to the 
text, then choose the image 
which best answers the question. 
This part of the exam requires 
candidates to listen for specific 
information.
Encourage your students to do 
the following when they approach 
this task:
• Read the question and 
look at the images for each 
question.
• During the first listening, 
listen for specific information, 
choosing the best option.
• During the second listening, 
check that the answer is 
correct, focusing on the key 
information in the text.

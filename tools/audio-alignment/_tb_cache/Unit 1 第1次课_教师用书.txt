Warmer
Write the alphabet in a column on 
the board. Divide the class into two 
teams and give each team a different 
colour pen. Teams have five minutes 
to think of a country (in English) for 
each letter. When time is up, teams 
take turns to write the name of a 
country beginning with A on the 
board. When they have run out of 
ideas for A, move on to B. Do the 
same with C, D, E, etc. The winning 
team is the one with the most 
countries.

Culture notes
Ed Sheeran is a singer-songwriter 
from England. Born in 1991, Sheeran 
began recording music in 2004 and 
released his first collection of work, 
Spinning Man, independently. He 
released another EP in 2009 and 
by 2010 had begun to be seen 
by more people over the internet 
through YouTube. After signing with 
a record company, his debut album 
+ (pronounced ‘plus’) was released 
in 2011. The following year, he won 
the Brit Awards for Best British Male 
Solo Artist and British Breakthrough 
Act. Sheeran has sold more than 150 
million records worldwide, making 
him one of the world’s best-selling 
music artists.
Bruno Mars is a singer-songwriter, 
record producer and multi-
instrumentalist from the United 
States. Born in 1985 in Hawaii, his 
real name is Peter Gene Hernandez. 
He released his first studio album 
in 2010 and rose to fame. His third 
studio album, 24K Magic, received 
seven Grammy Awards including 
Album of the Year. Mars has sold 
over 180 million singles worldwide, 
making him one of the best-selling 
music artists of all time.
Blackpink are an all-female four-
piece pop band from South Korea. 
Their debut album Square One was 
released in August 2016. The first 
two singles from the album charted 
at number one and two on the 
Billboard World Digital Songs

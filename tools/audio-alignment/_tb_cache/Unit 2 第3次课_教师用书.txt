Culture notes
Camila Cabello is a Cuban-American singer-songwriter. Born in 1997, her real 
name is Karla Camila Cabello Estrabao. She rose to fame as a member of the 
girl group Fifth Harmony, which formed on The X Factor USA in 2012. Her debut 
studio album, Camila was released in 2018.
5 Seconds of Summer (often shortened to 5SOS) are an all-male four-piece pop-
rock band from Sydney, Australia. They formed in 2011 and originally rose to 
fame via YouTube. They released their fourth studio album in March 2020.

Valentina Tereshkova was born in Russia on 6th March 
1937. Her father was a driver and her mother was a factory 
worker. Valentina was a worker in a factory, too. Her hobby 
was skydiving and it was her dream to be an astronaut and 
to go into space.
In 1962, there was a big competition to find new astronauts. There were 
400 people interested in going to space. The training programme wasn’t 
very easy, but Tereshkova was hard-working. And she was the lucky one. 
Tereshkova’s big day was 16th June 1963, and she was ready.
The name of her spacecraft was Vostok 6, and Tereshkova was the first 
woman in space. The flight was very difficult because there were many 
technical problems and Tereshkova wasn’t very well for most of the flight. 
She was in space for three days. She is the only woman to fly into space/uni00A0alone. 
After that, she was very famous all over the world. Her face was on stamps in 
many countries.
In the year 2000, there was a big celebration in London, and Valentina 
Tereshkova was named the ‘Woman of the Century’.
At the opening ceremony of the Winter Olympics in Russia in 2014, 
Valentina Tereshkova was one of the carriers of the Olympic flag. 
These were very important moments for her.

Activity idea Extension
Students could role play an interview 
with Valentina Tereshkova. Divide the 
class into two groups, A (Valentina) 
and B (Reporter). Ask group B to look 
at the article again and write down five 
questions for Valentina (in the present 
tense or using can – Can you still 
skydive?). Ask group A to look at the 
article again and find key information 
in preparation for an interview. Put the 
students in AB pairs and give them 
time to act out the role play.
Culture notes
Martin Luther King Jr. was an activist 
and leader in the American Civil 
Rights Movement in the 1950s and 
60s. His ‘I Have a Dream’ speech 
is one of the most recognised of 
the period. In 1964, King became 
the youngest person to win the 
Nobel Peace Prize for combating 
racial inequality through nonviolent 
resistance. King was fatally shot on 
4 April, 1968 in Memphis, United 
States. In 1971, Martin Luther King 
Jr. Day was established as a holiday 
in cities and states throughout the 
US. It is observed each year on the 
third Monday of January.
Diana, Princess of Wales (born 
Diana Spencer) was a member of 
the British royal family. She became 
well-known with her engagement 
to Prince Charles, the eldest son of 
Queen Elizabeth II, but their 15-
year marriage ended in divorce in 
1996. Diana was renowned for her 
unconventional approach to charity 
work, centring on children and young 
people, cancer and AIDS patients, 
and people affected by mental 
illness. She also campaigned for the 
removal of landmines. Diana died in a 
car crash in Paris in 1997, while trying 
to escape paparazzi. 
Valentina Vladimirovna Tereshkova is 
a former cosmonaut, engineer and 
member of the Russian State Duma. 
She is the only woman to ever have 
flown in space on a solo mission. 
On 16 June, 1963, she spent almost 
three days in space and orbited Earth 
48 times on the Vostok 6. She retired 
from the Air Force in 1997 with the 
rank of major general.

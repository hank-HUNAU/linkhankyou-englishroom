1 In A2 Key for Schools Reading 
and Writing Part 3, candidates 
have to read a longer text, e.g. a 
simplified newspaper or magazine 
article, and choose the correct 
option, A, B or C, in five multiple-
choice questions about the 
text. This part of the exam tests 
understanding of the main ideas 
and key details in longer texts.
Encourage your students to do 
the following when they approach 
this task:
• Skim the text to have a sense 
of the topic and general 
meaning.
• Read the text more 
carefully.
• Compare each question with 
the text and choose the best 
option.
• Check the chosen answer 
carefully against the text. 
Check the other two options 
and decide why they are 
wrong.

2  6.11 In A2 Key for Schools 
Listening Part 5, candidates have 
to listen to a longer conversation 
between two people who know 
each other, and match two lists 
of items by identifying simple 
information in the conversation. 
This part of the exam tests 
understanding of detailed 
information.
Encourage your students to do 
the following when they approach 
this task:
• Read and listen to the 
instructions, then read the 
questions and think about the 
context.
• Notice that all the words in 
each list are connected (for 
example here, people, and 
words to do with faces).
• On the first listening, tell 
students to get the gist and 
choose the best option for 
each question.
• On the second listening, 
students check all the answers 
carefully, focusing on specific 
information.

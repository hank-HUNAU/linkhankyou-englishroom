Scotland is the UK’s most northerly country. It occupies approximately one-third 
of the island of Great Britain. The land was settled by the Celts from Ireland in 
around the 5th century CE. As well as the mainland, Scotland includes more than 
790 islands, mainly within the Northern Isles and the Hebrides archipelagos. The 
capital city is Edinburgh (pronounced (/ˈɛdɪnbərə/) and in 1999 a new Scottish 
Parliament – the first since 1707 – was elected and given significant powers over 
Scottish affairs.
Fort William is a town in the Highlands of Scotland. It lies at the foot of Ben 
Nevis, Britain’s highest mountain, and has a population of around 10,500.
Mallaig (pronounced /ˈmælɪɡ/) is a small port village on the west coast of the 
Highlands of Scotland. The local railway station is the end of the West Highland 
railway line.
The Jacobite is a tourist train service that operates by steam locomotive-hauled 
train over part of the West Highland Line in Scotland. It covers a distance of 
about 66 km between Fort William and Mallaig and has been in operation every 
summer since 1984. It plays an important role in supporting the scenic route and 
although not the same train, the route across the Glenfinnan Viaduct is the one 
shown in the Harry Potter films.

Culture notes
The Danube is the second-longest 
river in Europe. Originating in 
Germany, it flows through ten 
countries in Central and Eastern 
Europe. The Danube has been a 
traditional trade route in Europe 
since ancient times. It is also an 
important source of hydropower and 
drinking water.
Budapest is the capital city of 
Hungary. It is the ninth-largest city by 
population in the European Union. 
The city spans the river Danube (in 
Hungarian: Duna) and consists of 
two parts, Buda and Pest, which 
are situated on opposite sides 
of the river. It has been settled 
since prehistoric times, and was 
designated a UNESCO World 
Heritage Site in 1987, particularly for 
the areas around the Danube and 
Buda Castle.

the journeys. Ask a representative of each group to report back their conversation 
during feedback. Have a show of hands to decide which is the best journey.

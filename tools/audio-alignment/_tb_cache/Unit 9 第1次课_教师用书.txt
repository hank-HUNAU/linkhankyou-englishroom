Warmer
Ask students to draw three pictures 
of food: one of a meal they ate 
yesterday, another of their favourite 
meal and another of their least 
favourite meal. In pairs, students 
take turns to guess which picture 
is which, e.g. I think that is your 
favourite meal. Students will not have 
learned the past tense yet, so clarify 
the meaning of ate by gesturing to 
yesterday. Ask for examples in open 
class. Make a note of any food items 
mentioned on the board.

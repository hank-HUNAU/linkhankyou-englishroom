TIPS FOR THINKING ABOUT 
OTHER PEOPLE
• Think about how your actions can change 
other people’s feelings.
• Remember to ask how other people feel.
• Ask yourself: ‘Does this person need help?’ 
or ‘Is what I am doing OK for them, too?’

Nikolai Kutsenko, from Ukraine, can do amazing things with a football. He can kick 
a/uni00A0ball well … but he can also keep the ball in the air with his feet, legs and head. Lots of 
footballers can do this. But can they do it for 24 hours and 30 minutes without stopping? 
Nikolai can and it’s a world record!
Sloan Henderson, from Utah, is a really good snowboarder/uni00A0 – and she’s only 
14 months old! Her mother and father take her to the mountains, and they put her on 
a snowboard – and there she goes! Sloan loves to be on the board. People are often 
surprised when they see her because she doesn’t fall over!
American rock-climber Maureen ‘Mo’ Beck was born without her left hand and only part 
of her left arm, but she can climb really well. She is the winner of two climbing World 
Championships for disabled athletes, and five national championships. She has her 
own special way of climbing, but Maureen thinks she is just the same as any other climber.
Goatee can’t talk or write, but she can surf! Why is this special? Because Goatee is a goat! 
She surfs with her owner, Dana McGregor. Dana helps Goatee to stand on the board and 
together they ride the waves in California. Goatee wears a yellow life-jacket because she can’t 
swim very well! And now there is a children’s book about her, too – Dana is the author.

Activity idea Extension
Divide the class into two groups, A and B. Tell students they are going to 
prepare a role play. A students love sport and have to convince B students to 
do sport. B students don’t like sport at all. A students work together in pairs 
to think of reasons to convince B to do sport. B students work in pairs to think 
of reasons not to do sport. After five minutes of preparation time, regroup 
students in AB pairs for them to do the role play. Monitor and make a note 
of any errors, but also note good use of language to praise during feedback. 
During whole-class feedback, encourage further discussion.

Culture notes
Nikolai Kutsenko juggled a football 
for 24 hours 30 minutes nonstop 
with his feet, legs and head on 6 
December, 1995 in Kiev. A similar 
record is held by Dan Magness of 
England who kept a football in the air 
for 26 hours using just his feet, legs, 
shoulders and head in June 2010.
Sloan Henderson is from North Salt 
Lake in the United States. She made 
the news in 2016 when at just 14 
months of age, her mother posted 
a video of her riding the slopes of 
Park City Mountain Resort on a 
snowboard.
Maureen Beck is a paraclimbing 
champion from Maine in the US. 
Beck climbed for the first time 
when she was 12. Since becoming 
a competitive climber, Maureen has 
won five national titles and gold 
medals at the 2014 Paraclimbing 
World Championships in Spain and 
the 2016 World Championships in 
Paris. 
The IFSC Paraclimbing World 
Championships are held every two 
years and are organised by the 
International Federation of Sport 
Climbing (IFSC). They are the world 
championships for competition 
climbing for people with disabilities. 
The first event was organised in 
Arco, Italy in 2011.

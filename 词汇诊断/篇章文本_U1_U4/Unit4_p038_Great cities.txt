Great cities
Istanbul, Turkey The wonderful city of Istanbul is a great place to visit. It is an old city, but there are some modern things to see, too. You can visit the beautiful Topkapi Palace and then go to Sultanahmet Square. On one side of the square is the Blue Mosque, and, on the other side, the museum of Hagia Sofia. Take a boat trip on the Bosphorus – the water between the two sides of the city. Then stop and have coffee – there are many cafés in the city. Are there any good places to eat? Of course! There are many restaurants, and you can eat great fish for lunch or dinner at Kumkapi Market. Nara, Japan Nara is an old city in Japan, about 400 km from Tokyo. It isn’t very big – about 400,000 people live there – but it is famous for many things. Many visitors go to see the Todai-ji Temple, a huge wooden building. There is a statue inside. It’s over 15 metres tall! In the city there is a park with hundreds of deer. There aren’t any walls in the park, so the deer can walk anywhere in the city. Buy some special food and give it to them – they love it!

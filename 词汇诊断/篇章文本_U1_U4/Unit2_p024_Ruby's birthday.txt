Ruby's birthday
Hi, Ben, how’s it going? Hey! I’m fine thanks. What about you? I’m really excited. It’s my birthday. Your birthday? Today? Isn’t it next week? No, it’s today. So where’s my present? Umm, err. Yes, your present. What do you want? I don’t know. A surprise? How about tickets for the Camila Cabello concert in June? Wow – that’s an expensive present. Camila Cabello? No, I don’t like her. What about a book about football? Football? Sorry, I don’t like it. I only like tennis. OK, I know. A 5 Seconds of Summer T-shirt. No, thanks. I don’t like them any more. Oh. Can I take you for an ice cream in town? There’s a great new Italian ice cream shop. Sorry. I don’t like ice cream. This is difficult. No concert, no book, no T-shirt, no ice cream. How about a big bar of chocolate? No? You don’t like chocolate? Of course I like chocolate. You do? But not milk chocolate. I only like white chocolate. Only white chocolate. OK. . I’m only joking. I don’t need a present. Really? Yes, and one more thing … What’s that? It isn’t my birthday today. It’s next week.

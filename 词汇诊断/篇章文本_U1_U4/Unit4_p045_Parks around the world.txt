Parks around the world
Villa Borghese is a big public park. There are beautiful gardens, trees and some fountains, too. People also call it ‘the park of museums’ because there are many interesting museums there. B Golden Gate Park, San Francisco, US Everyone knows the Golden Gate Bridge, but did you know that there is a Golden Gate Park in San Francisco, too? It’s very big (4.8km long, almost 1km wide) and at one end you can see the sea. C Chapultepec Park, Mexico City, Mexico Chapultepec Park is an important green space in this big city. It has a lake and a hill with a castle on it. People in Mexico City love going there. D El Parque del Buen Retiro, Madrid, Spain In Madrid there is a very big park called Buen Retiro. There are trees and paths and a lovely rose garden, too. There is also a beautiful glass palace. E The Iguana Park, Guayaquil, Ecuador The park’s real name is Parque Seminario, but everyone calls it the Iguana Park because it is full of iguanas. The iguanas are very friendly. People go there and feed them. F Mount Faber Park, Singapore On a mountain near Singapore, there is a park called Mount Faber. You can get there by cable car. In the park you can walk on a long path, or on an amazing bridge. You get a great view of the city.

Listening
Mike: Hi, Julia.
Mike: It's me, Mike.
Julia: Hi, Mike.
Mike: How's it going?
Mike: Do you want to go cycling later today?
Julia: Go cycling?
Julia: Good idea.
Julia: What time?
Mike: How about after lunch?
Mike: Say, two o'clock?
Julia: Two o'clock? No, sorry, I can't.
Julia: I've got a taekwondo lesson at two.
Mike: OK, how about three o'clock?
Julia: No, the lesson only finishes at half past three.
Mike: OK, so how about four o'clock?
Julia: I can't.
Julia: Mum wants to give me a tennis lesson at four.
Mike: Until what time?
Julia: I'm not sure.
Julia: Maybe about quarter to five?
Mike: Really?
Mike: But we can't go cycling then.
Mike: It gets dark at half past four.
Julia: Well, yes, you're right.
Julia: Sorry.
Mike: So maybe we can go cycling before lunch?
Julia: Um, I've got a school rugby match until about half past eleven.
Mike: What time does it start?
Julia: Ten o'clock.
Mike: Julia, it's quarter to ten now.
Julia: Really?
Julia: I need to go.
Mike: Let's go cycling tomorrow.
Julia: OK.
Mike: Call me in the morning.
Mike: See you.
Mike: Have a good match.

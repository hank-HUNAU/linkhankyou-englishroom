Extinct animals
1. The Great Auk
The Great Auk was a large seabird that lived in the North Atlantic. It was a bird but it couldn't fly. It could swim underwater really well. It caught and ate fish when it dived under the water. Its feathers were very soft and people used them to make pillows for their beds. Because of this people killed the birds. The last Great Auk died on the island of Eldey in 1835.
2. The Tasmanian Tiger
The Tasmanian Tiger was only found in Australia, Tasmania and New Guinea. It got its name because it had stripes like a tiger but it was not a member of the cat family. Farmers believed Tasmanian Tigers killed their sheep so they paid people to kill them. Many of the tigers died after getting ill from diseases they caught from other animals. The last Tasmanian Tiger died in Hobart Zoo in 1936.
3. Steller's Sea Cow
Steller's Sea Cow was a large animal that lived in the cold Arctic waters around Alaska. It grew up to nine metres long. Because it was so big it couldn't go under the water. It ate the plants on top of the sea. It wasn't a very dangerous animal. It was quite friendly. Because of this it was very easy to kill. Europeans first found the sea cow in 1741. 27 years later they killed the last one.

Transport Around the World

People use different types of transport to get from one place to the next.
In Wuppertal, a city in Germany, the 'Schwebebahn' or suspension railway is very special.
The first track opened in 1901.
It is the oldest electric elevated railway with hanging cars in the world.
More than 85,000 passengers use it every day.
There are lots of special cars in Cuba, especially in Havana.
Most of them are very old.
But these yellow taxis are much newer.
They are called cocotaxis because they look like coconuts.
They are a great way of getting around.
Tourists love them!
Tourists also like riding down the hills in Madeira, Portugal, on the Monte Toboggan.
Originally, people used these sledges to transport food and other things.
Today, they transport people.
Two 'carreiros', or sledge drivers, run next to the sledge and keep it on the street.
They wear special shoes.
When the ride gets too fast, the drivers can use their shoes to slow it down.
This strange form of transportation is called zorbing or globe-riding.
And yes, a person is inside!
The first zorbing site was in New Zealand.
But now you can go zorbing all over the world.
Even on water.

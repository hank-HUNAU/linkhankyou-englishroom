Unit 2-Listening
Hello. Can I help you?
Hi. Yes, where can I find toothbrushes?
They’re just there on your right.
Oh yes. I didn’t see them. OK, I’ll take this one.
Is that all?
No, there’s this toothpaste, too.
OK. So that’s £4.30.
Here you are.
And that’s 70p change. Have a nice day.
Thank you.
Are you all right there?
Yes, thanks. I just want a cover for my new phone. Have you got this one in red?
Sorry, only black.
OK, black’s fine. I’ll take it.
Right. That’s twelve ninety-nine please.
Can I pay with my contactless card?
Of course. Just place it over here.
OK.
Great. That’s gone through. Would you like your receipt?
No, it’s OK.
Do you need any help?
Hi. These trousers – can I try them on, please?
Sure. What size do you take?
Not sure. 28 or 30.
And do you want them in red? We’ve got other colours, too.
Yes, I saw them but it’s the red ones I’m interested in.
No problem. So why don’t you try both sizes? The changing rooms are in the corner.
Thanks!
Can I help you?
Yeah. These football socks. How much are they? I can’t find the price.
Just a moment. They’re eight ninety-nine.
Fine. I’ll take them.
OK, is that cash or card?
Cash.
That’s great and here’s your change and receipt.
Thanks.

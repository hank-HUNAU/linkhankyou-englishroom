Unit 1-Listening
Ben! Why don’t you have a rest and stop playing for a while? I’ve got homework. Ben!
What’s up, Sally?
I’ve got English and Geography homework. And you’re playing the guitar – badly!
Well, it’s my hobby and I have a good time when I practise. I love it.
Sure. But right now?
Well, yes! I’m practising now, then I can do my homework later.
Well, can you do your homework now, please? The same time as me? And then practise the guitar later?
Erm … well, yes, why not? But help me with my Maths homework, OK?
Oh, all right!
Mum, can I take judo lessons?
Judo? Really? What for?
Well … it’s good exercise, and some of my friends are learning judo, too.
I think it’s a great idea.
Really? Judo for girls?
Mum!!
Yes, a friend of mine at work does judo. She loves it and she says it’s very good for making her strong and keeping her healthy.
That’s right, Dad. And maybe I can make some new friends, too.
Oh. Well. OK.
Thanks, Mum! Thanks, Dad!
Jade! I think that’s kung fu, not judo!
Harry! It’s time for dinner.
Come on, Harry. Dinner’s ready.
Just a minute, Mum. I’m finishing something.
Harry, it’s dinnertime. Come and eat, we’re having dinner!
OK, Mum.
You’re always on your phone. Playing games.
No, not games, Mum. Puzzles. I’m doing a crossword.
A crossword? Oh, I hate crosswords. They’re too difficult.
I love them. They’re great exercise for my brain.
Are they? Well, OK. You know, you have to look after your brain, right?
That’s right. Just like you look after me, Mum.

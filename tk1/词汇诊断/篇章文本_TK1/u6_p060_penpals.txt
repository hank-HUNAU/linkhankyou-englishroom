Magazine article: Life stories – Pen pals for years
This week, Paul (from Brisbane, Australia) and Annette (from Toulouse, France) tell us about their friendship on different sides of the world.
When and how did your friendship start?
Fifteen years ago! We were both fourteen.
My school in Australia and her school in France started a virtual exchange programme for people to write to each other over the internet.
I wanted to improve my English and he wanted to improve his French, so we contacted each other.
Did you become friends immediately?
Not really.
I thought he was OK but not special.
He made lots of mistakes in his French! Haha.
We corrected each other’s mistakes.
But I made more than her! She corrected everything, too!
Paul always helped me to improve my English, but you know, after a few months, we stopped worrying about writing in perfect English or French and just wrote to each other and talked about ourselves.
I began to see that he was a very nice guy, easy-going and cheerful.
Did you feel the same way, Paul?
Yes, I did.
She often made jokes that made me laugh.
She’s really funny.
And we saw that we had a lot of things in common – books we liked, films we liked, things like that.
He’s also very intelligent.
So, when did you actually meet face-to-face?
Last year.
We met in Singapore.
That’s right.
We wanted to meet up, but it’s a long way and the plane tickets are expensive.
So we decided to meet halfway.
We had a really great time together for about five days.
Which language did you speak?
Haha! Good question.
English, almost all the time.
Her English is really good!
He’s just being kind! But yes, we had a great time.
I think we’re going to be friends for life.
I hope we can meet up again soon.

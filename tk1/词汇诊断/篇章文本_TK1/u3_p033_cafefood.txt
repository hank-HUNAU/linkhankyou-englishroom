Listening: In a café ordering food
Hello. Can I help you?
Yes, I’d like a cheese and tomato roll, please.
Do you want the roll hot?
Yes, please.
Would you like something to drink?
Yes, I’d like an apple juice, please.
So that’s a roll and an apple juice. Is that all?
Er, I’d like some carrot cake, too.
OK, that’s £8 altogether.
Here you are.
And that’s £2 change. The roll will be a few minutes. We’ll bring it to your table.
Thank you.
Enjoy your meal. And what can I get for you?
I’d like the chicken and mushroom soup, please, and an apple juice.
OK. Anything else?
No, I think that’s all.
What! No cake? But you said …
I’m trying to be good. I only buy cake at the weekends.
Really?
Yes, I’m being careful with what I eat. Have you got a problem with that?
No, not at all.
OK, that’s £6.50.
Can I pay by card?
Of course. Just put it in here.
It’s done.
Thanks. We’ll bring the soup over in a minute or two.
Could you bring me a fork with it, too?
Sure.
A fork? What do you need a fork for? You can’t eat soup with a fork.
I’ve got my reasons. Don’t you worry about it.
Moments later …
Well, you’re right. This is a good roll. How’s the soup?
Delicious. Would you like to try some?
No, I’m OK. My cake looks fantastic, doesn’t it?
Yes, it does. Mmmmm … it tastes fantastic, too!
Hey, what are you doing?
Mmmmm. That is a good cake!
But you said, cake only at the weekends.
I said I only buy cake at the weekends.
And?
Well, you bought this cake. I’ve got no problem eating your cake.
And that explains the fork.
Yes, it does. Now hurry up and finish your roll before I eat all your cake.
